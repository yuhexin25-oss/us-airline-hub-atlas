    About this atlas

    This visualization presents a curated set of U.S. airline network hubs and focus cities. It uses FAA CY 2024 passenger boardings to scale the markers and BTS TranStats carrier data to inform the operating-airline details.

    FAA also uses the word “hub” as a passenger-volume classification. Here, “hub” describes an airport's airline-network role; “focus city” identifies an important operating base with a smaller connecting role. Major airline lists are concise summaries rather than exhaustive service rosters.

    Airline route lines are generated from a static OpenFlights snapshot of non-stop U.S. segments. They include destination airports beyond the labeled hubs, but are not a live schedule.
